Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a dish that combines pasta with minced meat and cheese. Usually, it's cooked in the oven with a fireproof glass pan.

  • Pasta
  • Minced Meat
  • Cheese
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Olive Oil
  1. Cook the meat for 15'.
  2. Place in the special pasta.
  3. Add salt, pepper and olive oil.
  4. Cook in the oven for 45'.
