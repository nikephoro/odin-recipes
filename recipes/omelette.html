Omelette

Omelette is the easy protein-friendly meal for your everyday hypertrophy needs.

Omelette's are easy to make. Just SMASH! some eggs to get going.

  • 3 Eggs
  • Bacon
  • Cheese
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Olive Oil
  1. SMASH! the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Stir.
  3. Add salt, pepper and olive oil.
  4. Put it in a pan. Make sure it's pre-heated.
  5. When it looks good, add bacon and cheese.
  6. Take it all out as soon as the cheese melts.
